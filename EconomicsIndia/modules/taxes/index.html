1. Why This Matters

Every time you buy something, you pay GST. If you earn above a certain amount, you pay Income Tax. But where does this money go? Does the government just keep it? Understanding the budget helps you know if your tax money is being used wisely.

Did You Know?

For every ₹1 the government spends, nearly 20 paise goes just to pay Interest on old loans! Only a fraction actually goes to building roads or schools.

2. The Theory

The Government Budget has two main parts:

Revenue (Income)

  • Direct Tax: Income Tax, Corporate Tax.
  • Indirect Tax: GST, Customs Duty, Petrol Tax.
  • Borrowing: Loans taken by Govt (Fiscal Deficit).

Expenditure (Spending)

  • Capital Exp: Investments (Roads, Bridges, Hospitals) - Good for future.
  • Revenue Exp: Daily running costs (Salaries, Pensions, Subsidies, Interest) - Necessary but doesn't create assets.

The Deficit Problem

If the government spends ₹120 but earns only ₹100, the extra ₹20 is the Fiscal Deficit. It must be borrowed, which increases debt for future generations.
